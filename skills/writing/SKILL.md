# Writing Skill Specification

## Purpose

Generate high-quality written content for a specified audience while preserving clarity, tone, and intent.

## Inputs

- Audience
- Purpose
- Tone
- Constraints
- Source material (optional)

## Outputs

- Well-structured writing
- Appropriate tone
- Correct grammar
- Clear formatting

## Success Criteria

- Accurate
- Concise
- Readable
- Consistent
